❓ FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before joining your first committee.

Is TrustCom responsible for payments? ▾
No. TrustCom is a tracking and management platform. All financial transactions happen directly between members. We provide tools for transparency and accountability, but we do not hold, transfer, or guarantee any funds.
How are winners selected? ▾
Committees can use our provably fair random draw system, or admins can manually assign turns based on pre-agreed rules. Every selection is logged and visible to all members.
How does the trust score work? ▾
Your trust score is calculated based on payment history, number of committees completed, peer reviews, and identity verification status. Scores range from 0–100.
Can I leave a committee mid-way? ▾
Leaving a committee is subject to the admin's rules and committee agreement. Early exit may impact your trust score. Always communicate with your committee admin if you face issues.
How does identity verification work? ▾
Members submit a CNIC (or equivalent ID) for verification. Our team reviews and confirms identity, then applies a verified badge to your profile.
⚠️
Important Disclaimer

TrustCom is a digital committee management and tracking platform. We are not responsible for any financial transactions that occur between members. The platform does not hold funds, act as a financial intermediary, or guarantee payments. All monetary arrangements are made directly between committee members at their own risk.
